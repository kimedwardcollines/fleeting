Vehicle Management Features

01

Real-time Status Tracking

Track vehicle status: Active, In Maintenance, or Out of Service with instant updates.

02

Automated Maintenance Alerts

Smart notifications when vehicles approach service intervals based on time or mileage.

03

Fuel Efficiency Monitoring

Track fuel consumption patterns and identify opportunities for cost optimization.

04

Complete Service History

Full maintenance records including service type, cost, mechanic, and mileage at service.

Driver Management Features

01

Driver Profiles & Licensing

Complete profiles with license tracking, expiry alerts, and contact information.

02

Performance Ratings

Track driver ratings based on trip completion, fuel efficiency, and safety records.

03

Trip History & Statistics

Complete trip history with distance traveled, cargo handled, and time on road.

04

Availability Management

Status tracking: Available, On Trip, On Leave, or Suspended with automatic updates.

Trip Management Features

01

Trip Planning & Scheduling

Create trips with origin, destination, distance calculation, and scheduled departure times.

02

Cargo Tracking

Record cargo type, weight, and special handling requirements for each trip.

03

Real-time Status Updates

Track trips through: Scheduled → In Progress → Completed (or Cancelled).

04

Fuel & Cost Recording

Automatic fuel consumption tracking linked to trips for accurate cost analysis.

Technology Stack

🐍

Django 4.x Backend

Robust Python framework providing secure authentication, admin interface, and ORM for data management.

🎨

Bootstrap 5.3 Frontend

Modern, responsive UI with clean design, mobile-first approach, and cross-browser compatibility.

🗄️

SQLite/PostgreSQL

Reliable database with seamless migration support for scalable deployment options.

☁️

Cloud Ready

Deployable on Render, Heroku, or any cloud platform with proper environment configuration.
